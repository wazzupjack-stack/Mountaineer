# ▲ Mountaineer

**A crash game set on the world's most dangerous mountains.**

Six peaks. Six math models. Each mountain's ceiling is its real elevation divided by 100. Each mountain's volatility is derived from its real fatality rate. Weather, temperature and hazard zones are modelled on actual conditions.

**[▶ Play the demo](https://wazzupjack-stack.github.io/Mountaineer/)**

---

## What It Is

Mountaineer is a crash game — the genre defined by Aviator, JetX and Spaceman. A multiplier climbs from 1.00×. You cash out when you choose. If the round ends first, the stake is lost. The outcome is set by RNG at round start.

The difference is that the theme does real work. In Aviator, there is no reason the plane leaves. Here, altitude *is* the multiplier and thin air *is* the risk — the metaphor explains the maths instead of decorating it.

> **Previous versions of this repo described Mountaineer as a "predictive market."** That framing was wrong and has been removed. The outcome is generated by RNG, not by an external event. It is a casino game and is documented as one.

## The Two-Axis Math Model

This is the core IP. Every mountain has two independent variables.

**Axis 1 — Ceiling.** Maximum multiplier = elevation ÷ 100.

**Axis 2 — Volatility.** Real fatality rate sets the instant-fall probability.

| Mountain | Elevation | Ceiling | Instant-fall | Summit temp | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kilimanjaro | 5,895 m | 59× | 2% | −18 °C | Calm equatorial grind |
| Aconcagua | 6,961 m | 70× | 4% | −30 °C | Viento blanco gusts |
| Mont Blanc | 4,808 m | 48× | 7% | −15 °C | Low ceiling, deceptively lethal |
| Denali | 6,190 m | 62× | 8% | −40 °C | Coldest peak, sub-arctic pressure |
| Everest | 8,849 m | 88× | 12% | −36 °C | Highest ceiling, jet stream |
| K2 | 8,611 m | 86× | 22% | −45 °C | Busts constantly, pays enormously |

**Why this is interesting:** K2 sits 238 m *below* Everest, so their ceilings are nearly identical — but K2 kills roughly six times as often. Two mountains with the same maximum that play like completely different games, for reasons grounded in real data.

**RTP is 97% on every mountain.** Volatility changes; expected return does not. Distribution:

```
P(fall immediately) = p0
otherwise crash ~ Pareto(α), capped at ceiling
RTP at target x = (1 − p0) · x^(1−α) ≈ 0.97
```

**Summiting.** Reaching the ceiling is the jackpot event. Rare, celebrated, and it solves the problem that mountains — unlike sky — have a top.

## The Accelerating Climb

The multiplier does not rise at a constant rate. Growth scales with altitude:

```js
frac = ln(mult) / ln(ceiling)
rate = growth × (1 + 4.2 × frac^1.35)
mult *= exp(rate × dt)
```

At base camp it crawls. Near the summit it runs roughly five times faster. The decision window narrows exactly as the reward grows.

## Real Climate

Each mountain's weather parameters drive the rendering:

- **Everest** — jet stream at 118 km/h sustained, near-horizontal spindrift, Death Zone banded in red at 8,000 m
- **K2** — heaviest storm on the platform, whiteout density, the Bottleneck marked at 8,200 m
- **Denali** — aurora bands (63° N), fine ice crystals, sub-arctic barometric pressure
- **Aconcagua** — viento blanco dust haze, arid Andean palette
- **Mont Blanc** — rapid alpine cloud build, Grand Couloir traverse marked
- **Kilimanjaro** — calm equatorial air, clearest sky, lightest snowfall

Sky darkens and stars emerge with altitude. Temperature drops in real time. Above 7,000 m an oxygen-deprivation pulse kicks in.

## The Climber

Articulated figure with alternating ice-axe swings and leg drive, rope trail marking the route taken.

The animation cycle **slows as the air thins** — laboured movement at altitude while the multiplier accelerates. Breath puffs appear above 35% altitude and blow with the wind.

## Features

- **Dual bet panels** — two independent stakes per round, separate auto turn-back targets
- **Auto turn-back** — set a target, the system exits automatically
- **Provably fair** — server seed hashed and published pre-round, client seed, incrementing nonce *(demo uses a stand-in hash; production requires SHA-256)*
- **Live climber feed** — simulated players cashing out and falling in real time
- **Round history** — last 24 crash points, colour-coded
- **Hazard zones** — Death Zone, Bottleneck, Grand Couloir and others marked at true elevations

## Business Model

**Path 1 — Social casino.** Free-to-play, virtual currency, no cash redemption. Revenue from coin packages and battle passes. Substantially lower regulatory burden; shippable now. Crash is underrepresented in social casino, which is dominated by slots.

**Path 2 — B2B content studio.** License the certified game to operators via aggregators on GGR revenue share. No player acquisition cost, no support burden. Requires GLI-19 certification, a licensing jurisdiction and aggregator integration.

Sequence is Path 1 → Path 2: ship the social version, gather retention and mountain-selection data, then take the working product *and* the metrics into licensing conversations.

## Compliance

Before anything touches real money: GLI-19 RNG certification, a licensing jurisdiction, responsible gambling tooling, age verification and KYC, and a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction market assessment.

**This is a prototype and this document is not legal advice.** Every path except pure free-to-play carries legal complexity that varies significantly by jurisdiction and requires a gambling lawyer.
